## The density

The 2D heightfield cannot make an overhang: if the top block is solid, every
block below it is solid. The field adds a 3D term:

```text
density(x, y, z) = (surface(x, z) − y) + 20 m · noise3(x / 64, y / 10, z / 64)
solid            = density > 0
```

The vertical wavelength (10 m) is much shorter than the amplitude (20 m), so
the density is not monotone in `y`. A positive noise lobe can hold rock above a
negative lobe: that is the overhang. The horizontal wavelength (64 m) keeps the
surface coherent, and the perturbation is bounded to ±20 m.

`OverhangField::density` returns the signed Q16.16 value, `solid` the
thresholded result, and `is_overhang` the signature (solid here, air directly
below). All are pure functions of the seed, position and surface.

## Arches

A second 3D component places an occasional arch on a 64 m x/z cell grid:

- 3% of cells contain one;
- a span 5–9 m long, 1–2 m half-width and 2–3 m thick, 6–9 m above the surface;
- the span rises up to three metres toward its centre;
- a leg at each end reaches down to the lowest perturbed terrain
  (`surface − 20 m`), so the span is supported even where the base density
  dips.

The arch is a density addition, not a new material. Its opening is the air
between the surface and the span. The 3 × 3 cell check keeps the horizontal
footprint stable across a chunk border, like a cave shaft.

## No floating rock

`prune_unsupported(solid, [nx, ny, nz])` treats the mask as
`x + nx · (y + ny · z)` and seeds a 6-connected flood fill from:

- every solid cell on `y = 0` (the ground layer);
- every solid cell on the four vertical side faces (the terrain continues
  outside the sampled window).

Every solid cell not reached is set to air. The function returns the number
removed and is idempotent, so the chunk builder can run it after the surface,
overhang and cave fill. In the field scan, seed 2 has a four-voxel lobe that
the first pass removes and the second pass leaves alone.

The fixtures exercise both directions:

- a **shelf attached to a wall** is kept;
- a **detached shelf** and an **isolated block** are removed;
- the **arch** keeps both legs and its span.

## Tests

Unit (`src/overhangs.rs`):

- the density is deterministic and never rises above the amplitude;
- the cell-hash arch component has a span, a leg and an opening;
- a floating block is removed;
- an attached arch is kept.

Integration (`tests/overhangs.rs`):

- the arch fixture has a solid span, an open passage, two legs and no floating
  cells; adding a block above the span removes exactly that block;
- an attached shelf survives and a detached shelf is removed;
- a 64 × 64 field scan has overhangs in the 0.01–5% band and at least one arch;
  pruning is clean and idempotent;
- the seed-2 field lobe is removed and the arch/overhang features remain;
- the field uses the supplied surface (low and high masks differ).
