# M2.5-14 3D cave carve

**Status:** landed 2026-11-01. `crate::caves` adds the deterministic 3D cave
field: spaghetti tunnels, cheese chambers, vertical shafts, walkable galleries
and water/lava fills, bounded to 2,000 m below the local surface with a 2 m
solid seal under every water body.

## Acceptance

| Requirement | Result |
|---|---|
| All five types present | four morphologies plus water and lava fills; checked per seed over four seeds |
| Balanced density | each morphology is 10–45% of the carved volume, total carve is 5–15% of the scanned underground, and water/lava each fill at least 5% |
| No caves in the first 2 m under water | a submerged column returns `None` for the top 2 m of its bed; a per-voxel test proves the seal removes exactly those 2 m and nothing deeper |
| Every query is local and deterministic | noise, cell hash and fluid tables are integer; the same `(seed, position, context)` gives the same voxel |
| Tests | `cargo test -p voxel-worldgen caves` — 4 unit + 4 integration |

## The five types

| Type | Field | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Spaghetti | two independent 3D noise iso-surfaces at 40 m | winding tubes where both sheets cross zero |
| Cheese | 2-octave 3D noise at 48 m above `0.39` | rounded chambers |
| Shaft | 24 m cell hash, 55% of cells, radius 2–3 m | narrow vertical cylinders from 3 m to 90 m below the surface |
| Gallery | 2D iso-line at 40 m, width `0.10`, plus a floor band | walkable horizontal corridors, 3 m tall, floor 20–44 m down |
| Water/lava | fill from the water and lava tables | fluid-filled voids of any morphology |

Spaghetti is the intersection of two zero-crossing sheets: one sheet is a wall,
the intersection is a tube. Cheese is the opposite regime, a low-frequency blob
threshold. Shafts come from a cell hash so the radius is controlled; the 3 × 3
neighbour check makes a shaft's horizontal cross-section identical across a
chunk border. Galleries are a
2D iso-line extruded through a 3 m floor band, so their floor is flat and their
height is exactly 3 m. Fluid is independent of morphology: any carved voxel at
or below the water table is water, and at or below the lava table is lava.

Measured over seeds 1–4 on a 128 × 128 m patch with a 48 m surface:

| Type | Share of carved volume |
|---|---:|
| Spaghetti | 20.9–41.7% |
| Cheese | 13.4–42.2% |
| Shaft | 14.8–23.7% |
| Gallery | 16.5–29.5% |
| **Total carved** | **7.0–9.7%** of the scanned underground |
| Water / lava fill | 47–53% / 10–19% of carved voxels |

## Safety rules

- **Surface:** nothing is carved above the local surface.
- **Floor:** nothing is carved below `y = 0`.
- **Depth:** nothing is carved more than 2,000 m below the local surface
  (`MAX_CAVE_DEPTH_M`), the M2.5-15 budget.
- **Water seal:** when `CaveContext::underwater` is true, the top 2 m of the
  bed (`WATER_SEAL_M`) stay solid. Oceans, lakes and rivers cannot drain into a
  cave directly underneath them; deeper voids still carve normally.

## API

```rust
let field = CaveField::new(seed);
let context = CaveContext::land(surface, water_table, lava_table);
if let Some(voxel) = field.sample(x, y, z, &context) {
    // voxel.shape: Spaghetti | Cheese | Shaft | Gallery
    // voxel.fill:  Air | Water | Lava
}
```

`CaveContext` carries the solid surface, the underwater flag and the two fluid
tables. The caller samples block centres in Q16.16 metres; the result is a pure
function of the seed, position and context, so splitting a volume into chunks
does not change a voxel. `CaveContext::submerged` is the water-covered
constructor and `CaveContext::land` the dry one.

## Tests

Unit (`src/caves.rs`):

- nothing above the surface, below the floor or beyond the 2,000 m budget;
- the water seal blocks the top 2 m and still carves below it;
- repeated samples are identical, and the seed is preserved.

Integration (`tests/caves.rs`):

- four seeds each produce all four morphologies and both fluids;
- each morphology is 10–45% of the carved volume, the total is 5–15%, and
  water/lava each exceed 5%;
- the seal test compares a dry and a submerged context: no submerged carve in
  the top 2 m, an equal count below (proving the seal removes exactly 2 m);
- every gallery run is at most 3 blocks tall, so the corridors are walkable.
