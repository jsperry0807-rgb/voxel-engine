# M2.5-21 flora placement

**Status:** landed 2026-11-02. `crate::flora` places the biome catalogue's
plants and trees, restricts ecotone species to softmax seams, and adds rare
30–60 m giants on a 32 km grid.

## Acceptance

| Requirement | Result |
|---|---|
| Species | all catalogue flora and ecotone keys resolve in the block manifest, including the M2.5-31 birch, spruce, fern, cactus and dead-bush keys |
| Moderate density | a 128 × 128 m scan of every land biome produces flora, stays under 75% cover, and every non-zero catalogue density appears |
| Biome-dependent | forest gets oak, taiga spruce, desert cactus, plains tall grass; non-tree biomes have no tree placements |
| Ecotones only at seams | a 10,000-column forest/taiga transect has birch only where the runner-up weight is at least 0.2; no biome interior draws an ecotone species |
| Rare giants | one per 32 km cell (inside the 20–50 km band), height 30–60 m, only in tree biomes |
| No water/cliff flora | sea-level columns and slopes above 0.45 produce nothing |
| Tests | `cargo test -p voxel-worldgen flora` — 4 unit + 5 integration |

## Scales

| Class | Grid | Density |
|---|---|---|
| plants (`tall_grass`, `red_flower`, `fern`, `cactus`, `dead_bush`) | 1 m column | catalogue density per column |
| trees (`oak_log`, `spruce_log`, `birch_log`) | 8 m tree cell | catalogue density per cell |
| giant trees | 32 km cell | one per cell where the biome has a tree |

The 8 m tree cell is what keeps a 0.08 forest oak density to about one tree per
800 m² instead of one per 12.5 m². A tree still gets a jittered anchor inside
its cell, and its normal height is 4–12 blocks. A giant is 30–60 blocks.

## Ecotones

The dominant biome is the M2.5-10 softmax winner. A column is a **seam** when
the runner-up weight is at least [`SEAM_WEIGHT`] (0.2), the middle of the
300–500 m blend band. Only a seam may draw from the dominant biome's `ecotone`
list, at an 8% chance:

- forest/taiga → birch;
- plains/desert/savanna → the ecotone dead bush.

A tree ecotone still has to land on a tree cell, so birch does not carpet every
seam column. The normal desert/savanna dead-bush entries remain ordinary
non-ecotone flora; the ecotone flag distinguishes a seam scrub from the normal
desert scrub.

## Giants

`giant_anchor` puts at most one anchor 8–24 km inside each 32 km cell. If the
anchor's biome has a tree entry, the placement is a giant of that tree species;
a biome without trees (plains, desert, tundra, ...) gets no giant. The test
samples 8 × 8 cells in a forest context and gets 64 giants, one per cell, all
30–60 blocks; the same anchors in a plains context produce no giant.

## Content

The catalogue already owns the species and ecotone lists; this task adds no
data. The integration test:

- collects every `flora.species` and `ecotone` key from `data/biomes.ron`;
- resolves each through the committed block registry with atlas materials;
- maps every tree log to its leaf key and resolves that too;
- asserts the M2.5-31 flora keys are actually used:
  `birch_log`, `birch_leaves`, `spruce_log`, `spruce_leaves`, `fern`, `cactus`,
  `dead_bush`.

## Tests

Unit (`src/flora.rs`):

- log-to-leaf mapping;
- tree anchors sit inside their 8 m cell;
- giant anchors sit inside their 32 km cell;
- normal tree heights are 4–12 and giant heights 30–60.

Integration (`tests/flora.rs`):

- every species/ecotone/leaf key resolves;
- per-biome 128 × 128 density scan: non-empty for flora biomes, at most 75%
  cover, tree presence matches the catalogue, every non-zero entry appears;
- forest/taiga transect: ecotones only when the runner-up weight is at least
  0.2; birch appears and is always ecotone; forest interior and desert interior
  draw no ecotone species; normal desert dead bush is non-ecotone;
- 64 giant cells in a forest context are giants with heights 30–60; plains
  giant anchors are not giants;
- ocean and steep-slope contexts are empty; repeated samples are identical.

## Files
