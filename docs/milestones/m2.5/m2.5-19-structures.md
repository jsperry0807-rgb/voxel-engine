# M2.5-19 structure anchors and prefab stamps

**Status:** landed 2026-11-02. `crate::structures` places one structure in a
2 km cell at a 15% chance and stamps four terrain-fitted prefabs: ruin, tower,
dungeon and bridge/road.

## Grid and anchors

The 1,024 km world divides into **512 × 512 cells of 2 km**. Each cell hashes
to a presence roll (15%) and a type. Present cells get an anchor jittered by
up to ±500 m around the cell centre:

```text
anchor = cell_centre + hash2(seed, cell_x, cell_z) mapped to [-500 m, +500 m]
```

Neighbours are 1–3 km apart, inside the design's 1–4 km band. Ocean anchors
are skipped, so the effective density is 9.7% of cells. The presence, anchor
and type are all pure functions of `(seed, cell)`.

| Kind | Footprint | Shape | M2.5-31 keys |
|---|---|---|---|
| tower | 7 × 7, height 17 | hollow stone tower, four plank floors, crenellations, west door | bricks, cracked, mossy, planks |
| ruin | 11 × 9, height 4 | broken wall ring, two gates, plank beams and an interior stone floor | bricks, cracked, mossy, planks |
| dungeon | 9 × 9, height 5 | buried stone room with walls, ceiling and a plank floor | bricks, cracked, mossy, planks |
| bridge/road | 15 × 5, height 2 | plank deck with stone end caps, posts and railings | bricks, planks, mossy |

Foundations use `voxel:cobblestone`, which is also an existing manifest key.

## Stamp algorithm

For a standing prefab:

1. Sample the heightfield under every column the prefab touches.
2. Set `base = max(ground) + 1`, so no wall or deck block can end up inside a
   rise in the terrain.
3. Place the prefab blocks at `base + local_y`.
4. For each foundation column, fill from `base - 1` down to `ground + 1`. The
   lowest foundation block is therefore directly above the terrain, with no air
   gap.

A bridge only lists its two end caps as foundation columns. Its middle span is
therefore above the valley by design, but it is connected to both supported
ends. The test uses a synthetic valley, verifies both end columns reach the
ground, verifies the deck crosses above the valley floor, and flood-fills the
placed blocks to prove the span is connected.

A dungeon is different: it has no foundation. It is placed at
`surface - height - 2`, so every block is below the surface and inside the
terrain. The test checks that no dungeon block reaches the surface.

## Content

All prefab blocks resolve through the frozen `blocks.ron`/`materials.gen.ron`
with atlas materials. The four M2.5-31 keys are exercised by every prefab in
at least one position:

- `voxel:stone_bricks`
- `voxel:cracked_stone_bricks`
- `voxel:mossy_cobblestone`
- `voxel:oak_planks`

The integration test also rejects duplicate block positions inside a prefab.

## Tests

Unit (`src/structures.rs`):

- anchors stay inside their 2 km cells;
- every prefab contains an M2.5-31 key;
- a tower's foundation columns touch the ground;
- a dungeon is buried below the surface.

Integration (`tests/structures.rs`):

- 25,454 structures in 262,144 cells, all four kinds present;
- adjacent anchors stay inside 1–4 km;
- every prefab key resolves in the manifest and has atlas materials; no
  duplicate prefab positions;
- on uneven terrain, every foundation column's lowest block is `ground + 1`,
  no block is inside the terrain, and every placed block is 6-connected;
- the bridge spans a valley on two supports and has no block in the valley
  floor;
- the dungeon is fully below the surface and its shell is connected.
