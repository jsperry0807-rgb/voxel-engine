# M2.5-17 ore placement

**Status:** landed 2026-11-01. `crate::ores` places depth-tiered coal, iron,
gold and diamond veins on a deterministic 16 m cell grid.

## Acceptance

| Requirement | Result |
|---|---|
| Metals + gems | coal, iron, gold and diamond all present; iron/gold are metals, diamond is the gem |
| Depth tiers | coal 30–1,200 m, iron 60–1,800 m, gold 300–2,200 m, diamond 900–2,600 m below sea |
| No surface gems | diamond stops 900 m below sea level; a scan above 5,800 m finds none |
| Vein continuity | capsules with radius 1–2 m and half-length 4–8 m; a 64³ scan has 5–8 components per ore, and every wholly interior component is at least 45 blocks |
| Every material key resolves | all four keys resolve in the committed manifest with atlas materials |
| Tests | `cargo test -p voxel-worldgen ores` — 5 unit + 5 integration |

## Depth tiers

Ore tiers use **sea level (6,600 m)** as the datum, not the local surface. The
world's land rises to 12,000 m, so a mountain's veins then sit deeper below its
surface; that is deliberate geological strata, and it keeps a vein's type
independent of which column happens to query it. Cave depth (M2.5-15) remains
local to the surface because caves are carved terrain, not a stratum.

| Ore | Height band | Depth below sea |
|---|---|---:|
| coal | 5,400–6,570 m | 30–1,200 m |
| iron | 4,800–6,540 m | 60–1,800 m |
| gold | 4,400–6,300 m | 300–2,200 m |
| diamond | 4,000–5,700 m | 900–2,600 m |

The bands overlap. A vein centre in the all-four band (roughly 5,400–5,700 m)
picks its ore from the cell hash; a shallow centre can only be coal or iron,
and a centre below the coal/iron floors can only be gold or diamond. The top of
the field is 30 m below sea level, so no ore is exposed on a normal land
surface.

## Veins

The field uses a 16 m cell grid. Each cell has a 35% chance of a vein:

- centre jitter 4–11 m inside the cell;
- radius 1–2 m;
- half-length 4–8 m along x, y or z;
- the capsule is solid ore for its whole length, so it is connected by
  construction.

A query checks the 3 × 3 × 3 neighbouring cells. The capsule can extend at most
6 m into a neighbour, so the neighbourhood is complete; a vein crossing a chunk
border is the same vein from both sides. If two veins overlap, the fixed cell
iteration order decides, which is deterministic.

The integration scan at y = 5,500 m measured:

| Ore | Blocks | Components | Largest interior component |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| coal | 536 | 6 | 143 |
| iron | 655 | 7 | 169 |
| gold | 927 | 8 | 117 |
| diamond | 499 | 5 | 169 |

That is 2,617 ore blocks in 262,144 voxels, **1.0% ore by volume**. Boundary
components are ignored for the size check because the scan clips them; every
wholly interior component is at least 45 blocks.

## No surface gems

Diamond's highest possible vein centre is 5,700 m, 900 m below sea level. A
capsule can add at most about 10 m, so diamond blocks stay below 5,710 m. The
integration test scans 5,800 m to 6,600 m and finds no diamond. It also checks
that the field returns `None` at sea level and above.

## Content

| Ore | Key |
|---|---|
| coal | `voxel:coal_ore` |
| iron | `voxel:iron_ore` |
| gold | `voxel:gold_ore` |
| diamond | `voxel:diamond_ore` |

All four are the M1.5-14 ore faces and already have manifest rows and atlas
materials. The test resolves each key through the frozen registry, so an ore
cannot drift out of the manifest.

## Tests

Unit (`src/ores.rs`):

- the field is deterministic;
- nothing is placed above the ore top or below the floor;
- the gem sweep above the 800 m depth line finds no diamond;
- the tier helper picks coal/iron shallow, gold/diamond deep, and nothing
  outside the bands.

Integration (`tests/ores.rs`):

- the all-four band has all four ores and 1.0% ore by volume;
- every interior component of every ore is a real vein (≥ 4 blocks, largest
  ≥ 8; measured 45–221) and the block/component ratio is at least 4;
- the band above the gem ceiling has no diamond;
- the deep scan has only gold/diamond, and the shallow scan has no diamond;
- every ore key resolves in the manifest with atlas materials.
