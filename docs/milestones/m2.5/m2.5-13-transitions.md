# M2.5-13 material dither and transition blocks

**Status:** landed 2026-11-01. `crate::transitions` adds the 1 m surface dither
and the canonical two-material + debris sub-block templates.

## Acceptance

| Requirement | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 m material dither | the surface palette is chosen per 1 m block from the cumulative land/ocean weights; the expected share equals the weight |
| Seam is mixed, not a line | a 601-column desert→savanna transect keeps both top materials on both sides of the weight crossover, with at least four switches in the ±100 m window |
| ≤ 4 variants per pair | `VARIANTS_PER_PAIR = 4`; variant numbers wrap, and the four patterns are distinct |
| Two materials + debris | each 16³ template has A, B and a debris role; debris is always a third material |

| Tests | `cargo test -p voxel-worldgen transitions` — 8 unit + 8 integration |

## Dither

The weight field varies over 300–500 m (M2.5-10). An `argmax` would draw a
one-block line through that band. Instead the surface palette is chosen per
1 m column by walking the cumulative surface weights:

```text
roll  = hash2(seed, block_x, block_z)      // uniform in [0, ONE)
index = first i where roll < sum(weights[..=i])
palette = catalog().biomes[index].surface
```

`choose_surface` excludes the underground and rare biomes and renormalises the
land/ocean weights, so a trace cavern or geothermal weight can never place a
cavern material in a field. `palette_for_block` and `top_for_block` are the
concrete 1 m entry points; `palette_at` takes a precomputed hash.

Because the choice is a weighted draw, the expected share of each palette is
its weight, and neighbouring columns interleave the two palettes. The
integration test samples 20,000 hashes at a desert/savanna midpoint and checks
every biome count against its weight with a 5σ + 20 tolerance.

## Transition templates

A transition block is a **16³ sub-block template** (4,096 voxels) inside one
1 m block. Every voxel has one of three roles:

| Role | Material |
|---|---|
| `A` | first parent key, on the high side of the axis (above for `Y`) |
| `B` | second parent key, on the low side (below for `Y`) |
| `Debris` | the first debris key that is not a parent |

`TransitionTemplate` stores the two parents, the debris key, the variant and
the axis. `role(x, y, z)` returns the pattern role; `material(x, y, z)` maps it
to a key; `counts()` returns the three totals. The template is a pure function
of those fields and allocates nothing.

### Four canonical variants

| Variant | Boundary | Shape |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | `x = 8` | straight |
| 1 | `8 + (u - 8) / 3` | gentle diagonal |
| 2 | `8 + (|u - 8| - 8) / 2` | V wedge pushing B into A |
| 3 | `8 + (5u + 3v) % 5 - 2` | two-row jitter |

Around the boundary (within one voxel) a deterministic hash of the coordinate
and variant turns roughly one fifth of the voxels into debris. The debris
keeps the seam from reading as a clean plane without swallowing either parent.

`variant_for(hash)` maps a per-block hash to `0..4`; a larger value wraps, so
there can never be a fifth variant. `TransitionAxis::X|Y|Z` rotates the same
four patterns: `Y` is the horizontal top/filler boundary, `X`/`Z` a vertical
biome seam.

### Debris

`DEBRIS_KEYS` is `gravel`, `cobblestone`, `clay`, `sand`. `debris_for(a, b)`
returns the first one that is neither parent. All four already exist in the
block manifest and have atlas materials; the template adds no key of its own.

### Column integration

`transition_for(&SurfaceColumn, hash)` returns a `Y`-axis template when the
column has a real top/filler boundary. A beach (sand over sand), a cliff
(stone exposed), a rock line and a one-block snow cap have no boundary to mix,
so they return `None`. A normal soil column such as grass over dirt gets one of
the four variants.

## Zero new atlas slots

The data files are untouched: no block row, no face and no `materials.gen.ron`
entry is added. The integration test enforces the consequence:

- every palette key resolves in the frozen block registry and has face
  materials;
- every debris key resolves and has face materials;
- every unordered pair's template emits only `{a, b, debris}`, and every one
  of those keys resolves;
- the four variants across all three axes emit only the three keys.

A template therefore reuses the parent textures and costs zero atlas slots,
which is also the M2.5-30/31 acceptance rule for transitions.

## Tests

Unit (`src/transitions.rs`):

- variants wrap at four;
- every variant has both parents and debris on every axis;
- `A` is above `B` on the vertical axis, so a soil column's top material stays
  on top;
- the four patterns are distinct;
- debris is never a parent;
- the surface chooser never returns an underground/rare biome;
- palette choice is deterministic;
- a soil column gets a horizontal template and a same-material column does
  not.

Integration (`tests/transitions.rs`):

- the dither distribution matches the weights (20,000 samples);
- the surface dither excludes underground/rare biomes across an axis grid;
- the desert→savanna seam is mixed, with both materials on both sides of the
  crossover and at least four switches;
- at most four distinct variants per pair, and variant numbers wrap;
- every palette and debris key has a manifest row and atlas materials;
- every pair's template resolves and every variant/axis emits all three roles;
- `transition_for` agrees with the column boundary rule for every biome;
- palette choice is deterministic.
