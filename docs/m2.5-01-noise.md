## Design

### Why two fixed-point widths

The world is 1,024 km per side. In Q16.16 metres that is `6.7e10`, which does
not fit in an `i32`, so **coordinates** are `i64` with 16 fractional bits
(48.16) and **values** are `i32` with 16 fractional bits (16.16), normalised to
`[-1, 1]`. Coordinate scaling (fractal octaves, lacunarity 2 or more) uses an
`i128` intermediate so a world-edge coordinate cannot wrap.

The lattice index is the arithmetic right shift of the coordinate and the
fraction is `value & 0xFFFF`. That makes negative coordinates sample the cell
below zero rather than wrapping; `coord_floor`/`coord_frac` tests pin the
behaviour at `-0.25`, `-1.0` and `-1.5`.

### Hash

Every corner hash is `splitmix64` over the seed and the lattice coordinate,
folding one axis at a time:

```
hash2(seed, x, y) = splitmix64(splitmix64(seed ^ x*KX) ^ y*KY)
```

Folding each axis and finalising before the next one matters: a single XOR of
two axis products is not injective and produced a collision in the first
implementation (two corners sharing a gradient). The test
`lattice_hashes_are_signed_and_distinct` covers negative and mixed-sign
neighbours.

There is no ordered RNG anywhere. Placement code will use `hash::unit(hash)`
for a uniform `[0, 1)` value, so a feature is a pure function of its anchor
cell.

### Interpolation

Corner gradients are dotted with the **raw** fraction and blended with the
quintic fade `6t^5 - 15t^4 + 10t^3`. Using the faded fraction inside the dot
product would flatten the field near every lattice point. The fade is
monotone, has zero first and second derivatives at both ends, and is tested at
65 steps.

All arithmetic truncates (arithmetic shift); nothing rounds. Rounding a
negative product and its positive mirror symmetrically needs a branch, and a
branch is the only place two builds could disagree.

### Normalisation is a proof, not a sampled maximum

The raw field is larger than one. The scale is `0.995 / B`, where `B` is the
convex-hull bound of the field:

| Dimension | `B` | Where | `NORM` |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| 2D | 1.0 | cell centre, 8-diagonal gradients | 65,208 |
| 3D | 1.03633 | `(0.3583, 0.4833, 0.5)`, cube-edge gradients | 62,932 |

Because interpolation is convex, `|v| <= sum(weight_i * |g_i . offset_i|)`, and
each corner gradient can be chosen to align with its offset. In 2D the offset
metric is the Manhattan distance to the corner and its maximum is 1.0 at the
centre. In 3D the gradient set has only cube edges, so the metric is the sum of
the two largest offset components, maximised at 1.03633. With the 0.995 factor
the `clamp_unit` guard cannot change a sample; `noise_normalisation_never_clamps`
scans six seeds over the negative half-plane and a 3D block to confirm.

Observed extremes are about 0.76 in 2D and 0.92 in 3D. Callers scale amplitude
explicitly (heights in metres), so the unused headroom costs nothing.

### Fractal sums

`fbm_2d`/`fbm_3d` take `octaves`, a Q16.16 `lacunarity` and a Q16.16 `gain`,
scale the coordinate per octave and divide by the total amplitude so the result
stays in `[-1, 1]`. They are the hook M2.5-03/04 use for continentalness,
erosion, climate and the 50–500 m detail octaves.

## Golden vectors

`tests/golden/noise.txt` stores one line per section: name, FNV-1a 64 over the
little-endian `i32` samples, then the first 16 samples. A mismatch names the
section and the first differing sample, so a failure points at a value rather
than only a hash. Sections cover:

- `value_2d`, `value_3d`, `fbm_2d`, `fbm_3d` for seeds 0, 1 and
  `0xDEADBEEFCAFEBABE`, over fractional, negative and mixed-sign grids;
- `value_2d/world_edge`, `value_3d/world_edge` at ±1,024,000 m with fractional
  offsets, which is the far end of the 48.16 coordinate range.

Regenerate only for an intentional contract change, and record it in the task
list and this document:

```sh
UPDATE_GOLDEN=1 cargo test -p voxel-worldgen noise
cargo test -p voxel-worldgen noise
```

## Throughput

Measured on the reference machine (Ryzen 5 7600), release build, 4 M samples
each, coordinates converted once per sample:

| Field | Throughput | Per sample |
|---|---:|---:|
| `value_2d` | 70.4 M samples/s | 14.2 ns |
| `value_3d` | 30.8 M samples/s | 32.5 ns |

This is orientation, not a gate. The 2 ms/chunk budget is M2.5-23; a chunk
samples 512 blocks and mixes several fields, so the noise cost is a small part
of that budget.
