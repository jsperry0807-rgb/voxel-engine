# M2.5-06 hydrology

**Status:** landed 2026-11-01. Priority-flood filling, D8 flow directions and
flow accumulation work on arbitrary heightfields and run over the full 64M-cell
world in 45 s.

## Acceptance

| Requirement | Result |
|---|---|
| Priority-flood depression fill | `Hydrology::compute`, Barnes–Lehman–Mulla flow-direction form |
| Lakes at spill level | every cell in the hand-checked bowl fills to the spill height 3 |
| Flow graph acyclic | every cell's arrows reach an outlet in fewer than `cells` steps, tested on random grids |
| Hand-checked fixture | 5 × 5 bowl and 4 × 3 slope with explicit expected flows and accumulations |
| D8 flow and accumulation | eight compass directions; reverse-pop order accumulation |

Verify with `cargo test -p voxel-worldgen hydro` (9 integration tests plus 2
unit tests).

## Algorithm

1. **Seed outlets** — every border cell and every cell at or below sea level.
   A border cell drains off the map, a sea cell drains into the sea.
2. **Priority flood** — pop the lowest `(filled, index)` cell. For each
   unvisited neighbour set `filled = max(height, popped level)`, point its flow
   back at the cell that reached it, and push it.
3. **Accumulation** — walk the pop order in reverse: each cell is complete
   before its parent, so `accumulation[parent] += accumulation[cell]`.
   `accumulation` starts at 1 per cell and includes the cell itself.

The filled surface is exactly the minimum, over all paths to an outlet, of the
maximum elevation on the path. That is the textbook definition of a depression
fill, and the tests check it against a brute-force minimax search on small
grids.

### Determinism

- Heap entries are `(u16, u32)` and `BinaryHeap` compares the index on ties, so
  equal elevations pop in row-major order regardless of heap internals.
- Neighbours are scanned in a fixed compass order (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW).
- Everything is integer; there is no float and no hash-map iteration order.

Two full-world runs produce the same hash, `85a7887eba5e31b8` (seed 1).

### Why the border is an outlet

The generated world is 35% ocean, but the square edge is not guaranteed to be
sea. A land border must still drain, so the frame is treated as an outlet — the
standard priority-flood rule. A world that wants a ring of ocean can lower the
border heights before calling.

## Fixtures

### Bowl with one spill

```text
5 5 5 5 5
5 1 1 1 5
5 1 1 1 3   <- the only low border cell; spill level 3
5 1 1 1 5
5 5 5 5 5
```

Nine interior cells fill from 1 to 3 (depth 2); every border cell keeps its
height and the spill cell is an outlet. Accumulation at the spill is 10 (nine
interior cells plus itself), and the sum of accumulation over all outlets is
25, the cell count.

### Monotone slope, no fill

```text
9 9 9 1
9 5 4 2
9 9 9 3
```

Nothing is filled. The tree drains `(1,1) → (2,1) → (3,0)`, so the flows are
`E`, `NE`, `Outlet` and the accumulations are `1, 2, 3`.

## Finding: the shape field drains as domes, not basins

The largest accumulation in the world is **48 cells (6 km of flow)**. For
comparison, a 1,024 × 1,024 synthetic plane drains to a single corner with
accumulation 1,023, and the algorithm reproduces that exactly. The hydrology is
not the limitation; the **height field is**.

`surface_height` makes land a dome: `base(continentalness)` rises monotonically
from the coast to the interior, and erosion only changes the relief amplitude.
Water therefore runs radially down the dome into the nearest coast, so basins
stay small and no river can gather a catchment. M2.5-07 needs catchments, and
M2.5-10/12 expect rivers and valleys.

The fix belongs to the shape layer, not to hydrology:

- add the design's local detail noise (50–500 m) plus cliff/plateau shaping so
  the surface has valleys and interior flats (design §4.2 step 4);
- give continentalness a low-frequency continent component (~1,200 km) instead
  of the current 400 km-only fbm, so the world has 1–7 continents rather than
  an archipelago.

Both change the macro field and therefore the M2.5-05 goldens and the world
hash. They are deliberately not folded into this task.

## What is not here

- **River and lake extraction** — M2.5-07 and M2.5-08 turn accumulation and
  filled surfaces into vector rivers and lake records.
- **Tile integration** — tiles still use the M2.5-05 continentalness ramp for
  ocean distance; the real distance arrives with the hydrology plumbing.
- **Parallelism** — the full pass is single-threaded and already inside the
  design's "minutes".
