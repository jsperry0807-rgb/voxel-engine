# Shape-layer fix: continents and valleys

**Status:** landed 2026-11-01, after M2.5-06. Corrects a unit bug in the
M2.5-05 fields and adds the continent and valley structure the world design
asks for.

## The bug

The continentalness and erosion scales were written as

```rust
const CONTINENTAL_SCALE: i32 = ((ONE as i64 * ONE as i64) / 400_000) as i32;
```

which is `10,737 / 65,536 = 0.1638` — a factor of **6.1 m⁻¹**, not
`1 / 400,000 m`. The intended wavelength was 400 km; the actual field was ~6 m,
i.e. white noise at the 128 m macro grid. The erosion field had the same
problem with a 2.7 m wavelength.

Symptoms before the fix:

- the world was a salt-and-pepper archipelago at every scale;
- hydrology's largest accumulation was **48 cells** (M2.5-06's finding);
- the ocean share swung from 35% to 70% with the seed.

`ONE * ONE / W_metres` cannot express wavelengths above 65,536 m: the correct
Q16.16 factor is `ONE / W`, and for `W = 400,000` that is `0.164`, which
truncates to zero. The fix is to sample in **kilometres**: divide the Q16.16
metre coordinate by 1,000 first, then `scale = ONE / wavelength_km`. `ONE / 400`
is `163`, and a Q16.16 coordinate of one unit is 400 km.

## The field change

| Component | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Continent | — | 2-octave fbm, 900 km base, gain 2.4 |
| Coastline | 4-octave fbm, 400 km (broken scale) | 3-octave fbm, 400 km, gain 0.9 |
| Ocean share | fixed bias | per-seed 35th-percentile calibration |
| Erosion | 3-octave fbm, 180 km (broken scale) | 3-octave fbm, 180 km |
| Valley detail | — | 24 km (3 octaves, 300 m) + 8 km (2 octaves, 120 m) |

The continent component decides where land is; the coastline component
roughens the shore without moving the continents. The valley detail is added
to `surface_height` and weighted by `RELIEF_WEIGHT`, so the ocean floor and
beaches stay flat; only the land interior gets valleys. Without it the land is
a dome and water drains radially to the coast with no catchment.

### Per-seed calibration

A 1,024 km world spans barely one 900 km wavelength, so the seed decides
whether the raw field sits above or below zero. A fixed bias therefore cannot
hold 35% ocean: the measured share ranged from 0.345 to 0.70. `MacroGenerator`
now samples its raw field on a 64 × 64 grid at 16 km and derives the bias that
puts the 35th percentile at zero. The bias is a function of the seed, not
stored data, so a tile still regenerates identically everywhere.

## Measured result

Reference machine (Ryzen 5 7600), release.

| Quantity | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Land structure at 16 km sampling | salt-and-pepper archipelago | 1–2 continents |
| Ocean share, seeds 1/2/3 | 0.351/0.693/0.695 (fixed bias) | 0.345/0.354/0.355 |
| Largest flow accumulation | 48 cells | **5,706,011 cells** |
| Lake cells | 0.43% | 4.32% |
| Hydrology pass (64M cells) | 45.4 s | 11.2 s |
| Macro generation (4,096 tiles) | 9.0 s | 14.4 s |
| Peak hydrology RSS | 1.21 GiB | 1.05 GiB |

The "before" ocean shares are from the scale-fixed field with a single fixed
bias: the share swung with the seed. The original broken field held 35% only
because its bias had been tuned on the noise itself; that number was
meaningless.

The macro world hash is now `5b17f74f2bef0481` (seed 1) and the hydrology hash
is `5648830836ddaba9`. Both are stable across runs.

The 4.32% lake share is mostly single-cell depressions in the valley detail.
M2.5-08 extracts lakes with a size filter, so this is a seedbed for real lakes
rather than a finished set.

## Guards

The change is covered by tests that would have caught the original bug:

- `macro_generator_noise_fields_have_kilometre_scale` — adjacent 1 km samples
  must be correlated: fewer than 20 continentalness zero crossings along
  1,024 km, and fewer than 40 half-range erosion jumps. With the 6 m field the
  counts explode.
- `macro_generator_land_is_continent_scale` — on a 256 × 256 grid at 4 km,
  every seed has 1–7 landmasses holding at least 1% of the land, and 30–40%
  ocean.
- `macro_generator_surface_has_valley_detail` — valley detail exceeds ±100 m
  on land, and is exactly zero on ocean/coast cells.
- Per-tile goldens (`tests/golden/macro_tiles.txt`) and the CI world hash were
  regenerated; the differences are the whole point of the fix.
