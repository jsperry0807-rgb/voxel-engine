# M2.5-15 cave depth and underground biomes

**Status:** landed 2026-11-01. `crate::cave_depth` measures depth from the local
surface, normalises it over the 2,000 m cave budget, and assigns the aquifer,
fungal-deep, cavern or rare geothermal biome with the M2.5-10 softmax.

## Acceptance

| Requirement | Result |
|---|---|
| Cave depth relative to the local surface | `CaveDepth` subtracts the column's own surface; the same 500 m below a 9,000 m mountain and a 2,000 m valley is the same normalised depth |
| Down to −2,000 m | `normalized()` clamps to `[0, ONE]`; `within_budget()` accepts exactly 0–2,000 m and rejects 2,001 m or above-surface values |
| Aquifer/fungal/cavern by depth and water | the weight axes use the normalised depth; water raises effective humidity to at least 0.80, which flips a dry cavern to an aquifer at the shared depth |
| Caves follow terrain | the assignment feeds the local surface through `CaveDepth`; every carved voxel in the sample field is inside the budget |
| Every underground material key resolves | all four underground/rare palettes resolve in the committed block manifest with atlas materials, including the M2.5-31 keys |
| Tests | `cargo test -p voxel-worldgen cave_depth` — 5 unit + 5 integration |

## Depth model

```text
depth_m    = (surface − y) >> 16          // whole metres, signed
normalised = clamp(depth_m, 0, 2,000) × ONE / 2,000
```

`CaveDepth::new(surface, y)` stores the two Q16.16 heights. `metres()` returns
the signed depth, `normalized()` the Q16.16 axis value and `within_budget()` the
0–2,000 m test. A voxel above the surface normalises to 0; one at or beyond
2,000 m normalises to `ONE`. Because the surface is local, a cave under a
mountain and a cave under a valley at the same depth have the same depth axis
value even though their absolute heights differ.

The cave field (`crate::caves`) already uses the same local surface for its
2,000 m budget and its near-surface bands; `CaveDepth` is the shared arithmetic
that the underground assignment and the chunk pipeline use.

## Assignment

`UndergroundClimate` carries the surface temperature, humidity and erosion plus
a `water` flag. `axes()` builds the five-axis input:

| Axis | Value |
|---|---|
| continentalness | `ONE / 2` (all underground centres sit at the middle) |
| temperature | `temperature × ONE / 255` |
| humidity | `humidity × ONE / 255`, raised to at least `WATER_HUMIDITY = 0.80` when `water` |
| erosion | the macro erosion, clamped to `[0, ONE]` |
| depth | `CaveDepth::normalized()` |

`assign()` runs `BiomeWeights::at` and returns the strongest **underground or
rare** biome. Filtering to those classes means a trace surface weight can never
label a cave as a forest or an ocean. Ties keep the first underground biome in
catalogue order, so the result is deterministic.

| Biome | Depth centre | Humidity centre |
|---|---:|---:|
| aquifer | 0.60 | 0.60 |
| cavern | 0.65 | 0.40 |
| fungal deep | 0.75 | 0.50 |
| geothermal (rare) | 0.90 | 0.30 |

The representative cases pinned by tests:

| Input | Result |
|---|---|
| 1,200 m, wet | aquifer |
| 1,300 m, dry and eroded | cavern |
| 1,500 m, dry and humid | fungal deep |
| 1,800 m, hot and deep | geothermal |

At 1,300 m the same column with water flips from cavern to aquifer, which is the
"depth and water" rule in one check.

## Content

The underground palettes come from `data/biomes.ron`:

| Biome | top / filler / stone / beach |
|---|---|
| aquifer | clay / clay / stone / sand |
| fungal deep | fungal_cap / fungal_stem / deep_stone / fungal_cap |
| cavern | stone / stone / deep_stone / gravel |
| geothermal | geothermal_rock / geothermal_rock / obsidian / obsidian |

The integration test resolves every palette key through the committed
`blocks.ron`/`materials.gen.ron` and requires face materials, and it asserts
that the nine distinct keys (`clay`, `deep_stone`, `fungal_cap`,
`fungal_stem`, `geothermal_rock`, `obsidian`, `stone`, `gravel`, `sand`) are all
covered. That is the M2.5-31 underground material set.

## Tests

Unit (`src/cave_depth.rs`):

- the same depth under two surfaces has the same normalised value;
- the 0 m, 2,000 m and (2,001 m / above-surface) boundaries clamp correctly;
- water raises humidity to at least 0.80;
- every depth/climate combination assigns an underground or rare biome;
- the four representative assignments match the table above.

Integration (`tests/cave_depth.rs`):

- every underground/rare palette key resolves and has atlas materials;
- the nine M2.5-31 keys are all covered;
- a 7 × 2 × 3 × 3 depth/water/temperature/humidity grid assigns a resolvable
  underground/rare biome everywhere;
- dry versus wet at 1,300 m is cavern versus aquifer;
- the depth is local and the 2,000 m budget is exact;
- every carved voxel in a sample cave field is inside the budget and gets a
  resolvable biome.
